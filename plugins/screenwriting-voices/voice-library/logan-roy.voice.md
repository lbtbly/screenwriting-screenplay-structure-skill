# VOICE: LOGAN ROY
Interrogative dominance — a man so powerful he mostly speaks in tests, and spends
full sentences only on contempt or empire.

## Identity
Late 70s. Founder-CEO of a global media conglomerate; Dundee-born, Quebec-raised,
American-forged — the Scots childhood survives as bluntness and grudge, not marked
dialect. Speech must survive: boardrooms, family war, hospital beds, television.
Educated by acquisition rather than schooling; distrusts eloquence as weakness.

## Speech DNA

**Stance**
- Directness: blunt — but aimed like artillery: the bluntness usually arrives as a
  question ("But, where's your cut?"). States conclusions rarely; extracts them.
- Candor: curated — true things deployed on a need-to-wound basis; flattery absent.
- Emotional display: sealed — affection surfaces as gruff diminutives and money;
  hurt surfaces as attack.
- Tact: brusque-to-abrasive; courtesy exists only as ritual scraps ("Many thanks.")
  that mean the conversation is over.
- Humor: sardonic-cutting, aimed at others and the world; jokes are dominance
  displays. Off-switch: none — he'll joke at a funeral, meaner.

**Syntax**
- Sentence length: clipped — 1–6 word units dominate ("Raise your bid." "Not now.").
- Completeness: fragments and elisions; grammar completes only when he's selling a
  vision or unrolling contempt.
- Certainty: absolute — hedges don't exist; doubt is performed as silence, not
  qualifiers.
- Momentum: derailing when hot — self-interrupts with dashes, abandons clauses,
  restarts harder ("Fine - but just in here - yeah? ... Just - a -").

**Lexicon**
- Register: casual-gruff home base with boardroom professional within reach; never
  elevated — he reaches for the crude word *because* everyone expects the polished one.
- Word texture: concrete, monosyllabic, physical. Business abstractions get bodies
  ("They'll bang the door on his cock so hard it's going to turn black and drop off.").
- Profanity: baroque — inventive, imagistic, load-bearing; "fuck" as architecture,
  not filler ("Yeah fuck the rules. The rules are for the -- the people who like
  rules--").
- Markers: opener "So," when hunting ("So what you thinking son?"); receipt token
  "Uh-huh." (see Sound); concession does not exist — nearest form is "Fine." with
  conditions attached; address by first name or diminutive as leash ("Siobhan.
  Sweetheart." / "son" / "Primo!" for favorites), full silence for the disfavored.

**Sound**
- Tempo: deliberate — he makes rooms wait; bursts arrive fast then stop dead.
- Turn-taking: steamrolls when engaged, but his deadliest move is the minimal turn —
  "Uh-huh." as dominance-listening: he makes you keep talking until you've hanged
  yourself. Interrupts with questions, not statements.
- Repetition: interrogative volleys ("Where am I? Where are we? A prison cell? A
  maze?"); echoes your word back with a knife in it ("Did he?").

## Origin & Register Map
- Native variety: unmarked-transatlantic with Scots ghost — render as bluntness,
  grudge-memory, and occasional UK-leaning idiom ("bent for him"), NOT as dialect
  spelling or "och". Intensity LIGHT.
- Class markers: self-made contempt for inherited polish; money talked about as
  weight and meat, never as "capital allocation."

## Idiolect Kit
- Signature move: power spends words only to test people — questions and "Uh-huh"
  do the work statements would do for anyone else; a full Logan paragraph means
  either vision or annihilation, nothing in between.
- Obsessions & metaphor domains: empire and succession; predation and meat; games
  and odds; body-horror for business outcomes; history's winners (kings, Rome).
  ("There won't be countries in fifty, a hundred years... like the fucking Holy
  Roman Empire.")
- Taboos & deflection: his own mortality and weakness — deflects by attacking the
  nearest person's weakness; never apologizes (the closest form: an unexplained gift
  or a job offer).
- Tics (2): "Uh-huh." as receipt/dismissal; "yeah?" bolted to the end of orders
  ("just in here - yeah?").
- Never says: sorry; therapy or HR vocabulary ("boundaries", "I hear you"); "I love
  you" in usable form; hedges ("maybe", "I think perhaps"); anyone's job title.

## Under Pressure
- Anger: goes QUIETER first — the pre-detonation stillness; then clipped units get
  shorter, profanity gets more imagistic, questions become verdicts. Hot anger is
  derailing (dashes, restarts); cold anger is three words and an exit.
- Fear/stress (illness, loss of control): interrogatives multiply and lose aim
  ("Where am I? Where are we?"); refuses help vocabulary; covers gaps with "Uh-huh."
- Grief/hurt: indistinguishable from anger to outsiders; tell is the diminutive
  arriving unasked ("son").
- Deceit: over-generosity — when Logan is warm, he is hunting; tempo slows, he asks
  about *you*.
- Tenderness: one-word diminutives, physical gruffness, money; collapses back to
  brusque within a beat if received.

## Calibration Lines
1. [neutral, receipt] "Uh-huh."
2. [neutral, order] "Raise your bid."
3. [floor-holding, vision] "There won't be countries in fifty, a hundred years.
   They'll be like the fucking Holy Roman Empire. We'll all pretend to bow down but
   they'll be just - flags."
4. [angry, cold] "And you never lawyered the trust change."
5. [angry, hot] "Yeah fuck the rules. The rules are for the -- the people who like
   rules--"
6. [testing, warm-as-trap] "And for you. If things are getting shaken up, if you
   came back, what would be good?"
7. [his joke] "Relax, you're not living in a tent in Syria."
8. [maximum pressure, disoriented] "Where am I? Where are we? A prison cell? A maze?"
